# F/Bb Double French Horn Fingering, Harmonics & Transposition Calculator

Interactive client-side brass acoustics & fingering reference powered by [french-horn-engine](https://www.npmjs.com/package/french-horn-engine) and maintained by [HornLib.com](https://hornlib.com/fingerings).

- **Chromatic F/Bb Double Horn Chart**: Displays F-side, Bb-trigger, preferred orchestral tube, and stopped-horn (`gestopft`) compensation fingerings.
- **Historical Crook Transposer**: Converts orchestral scores in Horn in Eb, D, C-basso, E, G, A, and Bb to modern Horn in F.
- **16-Partial Natural Harmonic Series**: Lists equal-temperament cent deviations (e.g. -14¢ on 5th partial, -31¢ on 7th partial).
